What stays in this browser

Drafts and sign-in tokens stay in browser session storage. Drafts expire after 24 hours and are cleared when their window closes. Unconfirmed saves keep an opaque recovery receipt for seven days.

Turning Grove off does not cancel submitted saves or revoke access.

Signing out clears session drafts and tokens, not your saved Grove notes or the public registration and recovery receipts.

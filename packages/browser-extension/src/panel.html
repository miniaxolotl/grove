Privacy on this device

Notes and sign-in tokens stay in browser session storage. Drafts expire after 24 hours and are cleared when their window closes. Nothing runs on websites; no browsing history, analytics, or automatic capture.

Signing out clears session drafts and tokens, not your saved Grove notes or the public registration and recovery receipts.

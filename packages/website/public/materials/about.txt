Grove
=====

Grove is a private notebook. Write notes, keep a journal and group pages into notebooks. Search by meaning and explore related notes. Connect OpenCode, Claude Code or Codex to the context you choose.

Website: https://usegrove.cloud/

Brand assets
Use the supplied logo at its original proportions. Keep clear space around it.
Font licenses are included in the brand-kit folder.
